Guidelines for a Physical Copy of a Release:

Audio:
  Requirements:
    Min. 5 Tracks / 20 minutes total for a CD
    Min. 6 Tracks / 23 minutes total for a Vinyl
  Limitations:
    Max. 25 tracks / 79 minutes total for a CD
    Max. 8 tracks / 23 minutes total per Vinyl side*
  Format:
    All audio files must be delivered in WAV format. Stereo at 44.1, 48, 88.2, or 96 kHz. 16 or 24 bit depth, min. length 10 seconds**

Art Assets:
  Required:
    Cover Art Back · 3426x3048px (CD/Vinyl)
    Cover Art Front · 3426x2048px (CD/Vinyl)
    Single Label · 2834x2834px (CD Only)
    Side A Label · 2834x2834px (Vinyl Only)
    Side B Label · 2834x2834px (Vinyl Only)
    Unified Landscape · 6992x2048px (CD Only)
    Split Landscape · 6992x3048px (Vinyl Only)
  Optional:
    Booklet Cover Back & Front 2:1 Ratio · 5812x2930px (CD/Vinyl)***
    Booklet Landscape OR Pages 9 & 10 2:1 Ratio · 5812x2930px (CD/Vinyl)***
    Booklet Pages 1-8 · 2906x2930px (CD/Vinyl)***
  Format:
    All image files must be delivered in PNG format

* = Each Vinyl has side A and side B. Total max. 16 tracks / 46 minutes total for a Vinyl.
** = Just in case you delivered your audio files in a different format for the Digital Release (like FLAC).
*** = If you want a booklet, all booklet assets must be delivered.
